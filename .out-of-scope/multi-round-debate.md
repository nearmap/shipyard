# Debating until the two sides agree

`sy:debate` runs exactly one exchange (opening, attack, rebuttal) and stops.

## Why

A debate that loops until the proposer and adversary agree has quietly settled the decision itself, which is the user's call. One exchange sharpens the disagreement; the synthesized findings go to the user to steer. The bound also keeps every plan's mandatory debate pass predictable in cost. See `agents/debate.md`.
